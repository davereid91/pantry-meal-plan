Check the delivered labels. Freeze raw chicken before its use-by date and follow any opened-pack instructions. Cook raw chicken within 24 hours of fully defrosting. Reheat cooked portions once, until steaming hot. The salmon must be used on the day it defrosts.

Monday 28 SeptemberTap to view meals

Breakfast

Dave: Porridge with banana, blueberries, chia, flaxseed mix, almonds & honey

  • 250 ml Whole milk
  • 80 g porridge
  • 1 piece Banana
  • 5 g Chia seeds
  • 5 g Milled flaxseed, sunflower & pumpkin seed mix
  • 25 g Almonds
  • 50 g Blueberries
  • 5 g Honey

Warm the milk and porridge until soft, then add the banana, blueberries, seeds, almonds and honey.

Monday oats: 60 g plain oats + 20 g bought multi-seed porridge. On Tuesday–Sunday, use 80 g multi-seed porridge each day.

Sarah: Fage yoghurt with blueberries & flaxseed mix

  • 150 g Greek yoghurt
  • 20 g Blueberries
  • 20 g Milled flaxseed, sunflower & pumpkin seed mix

Stir the blueberries and flaxseed mix into the yoghurt.
