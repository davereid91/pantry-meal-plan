Prep & freezer

Sunday batch quantities and dated freezer reminders. Keep reserve portions frozen until their meals are confirmed.

Check the delivered labels. Freeze raw chicken before its use-by date and follow any opened-pack instructions. Cook raw chicken within 24 hours of fully defrosting. Reheat cooked portions once, until steaming hot. Allow portions to thaw fully and use within 24 hours of fully defrosting, or the shorter pack limit.

  • Freeze the new 500 g raw beef mince pack, labelled Thu 8 October dinner.Freeze beef
  • Keep salmon refrigerated for Monday and all four ramen components refrigerated for Tuesday, within delivered use-by dates. If duck or vegetables expire earlier, swap the two dinners only if the salmon date permits Tuesday.Check dates
  • Cook Sunday’s soy-ginger chicken dinner using the thawed existing 400 g portion. Eat half; cool and refrigerate the other half for Dave’s Monday lunch.Save half
  • Freeze the spare fourth bagel on delivery if its label permits; the other three are for Sarah’s Tuesday–Thursday lunches and must keep within the pack directions.Bagels

Sunday night 4 October

  • Move the existing frozen 2-pint milk bottle to the fridge for early-week use; allow it to thaw fully. Use the unfrozen milk first, then thawed milk within its actual label instructions. If it will not last until Wednesday, use the new bottle earlier.Thaw milk
