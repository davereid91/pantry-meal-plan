Prep & freezer

Tuesday batch prep and dated freezer reminders for Tuesday 13–Sunday 18 October.

Check the delivered labels. Keep raw chicken refrigerated and follow its label. Freeze raw portions on purchase day; fully defrost in the fridge and cook within 24 hours. Cool cooked rice quickly, ideally within one hour, then refrigerate or freeze promptly. Actual chicken pack directions take priority: published M&S instructions say do not reheat. Check those directions before storing or serving cooked portions.

Meals & snacks

Breakfast, lunch and snack weights are per person. Dinner ingredients make one whole recipe batch. Dave’s prepared lunches start Wednesday; Friday lunch uses Thursday’s spare dinner portion. Sarah has lunch Thursday and is out for dinner.

Sweet potatoes: the two orders contain 1,350 g against 1,400 g in the recipes. To fit those purchases, the suggested adjustment is 375 g in each Friday and Saturday dinner, keeping Sarah’s three lunches at 200 g. The recipe quantities below remain 400 g until that adjustment is agreed.

Corn: the six large cobs are for Thursday, Friday and Saturday dinner batches; the separate cobettes are for Sarah’s Friday–Sunday lunches.
